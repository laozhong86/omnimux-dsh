About TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas

Last updated: June 2026

Transform episodic storytelling into incremental growth with TikTok Growth Max - a performance advertising solution that converts in-platform content experiences into scalable and incremental business growth.

Content partners can publish Mini Dramas in TikTok, driving users from discovery to conversion all without having to leave the TikTok App. After publishing content, advertisers can set up content monetization and subsequently leverage TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas to promote content to incremental audiences while optimizing towards in-app ads revenue or in-app purchase outcomes.

Benefits of TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas

Frictionless experience: Target high-intent users with episodic storytelling entirely within TikTok, eliminating drop-off from additional redirects out of TikTok.

Scalable ROAS growth: Closed-loop ecosystem and rich onsite signals enables efficient optimization toward ad revenue (IAA) or purchase revenue (IAP), driving measurable ROAS from the start.

Target ROAS (Day 0): Achieve precise control by optimizing campaigns towards a set Day 0 ROAS goal, ensuring spend efficiency from the start.

Highest Value (Day 0): Maintains full and consistent daily spending to maximize D0 ROAS.
