### Global Scale & Audience Engagement

With advanced recommendation engine and massive user base, TikTok is committed to prioritizing resources to drive further growth for outstanding content partners.

### All-in-one Platform Support

TikTok Drama Center covers the entire chain of onboarding, series management, multilingual translation and revenue sharing, helping partners enter global content markets efficiently.

### High-Value Revenue Multipliers

Premium content, including localized international dramas, top-tier trending series, and AI-generated adaptations, is eligible for higher revenue incentives and dedicated resource packages.

### Integrated Monetization Management

Offering diversified commercial models and platform-driven management tools, TikTok Short Drama allow partners to seamlessly navigate different models to maximize content value.
