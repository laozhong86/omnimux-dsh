FAQ

How is OmniMux different from Buffer or Blotato?

They are tools for humans. OmniMux is the all-in-one social media API for AI agents.
Keep the agent you already use; we give it hands to create and orchestrate.

Can I use it without a technical background?

Yes. Send our URL to your agent, and it will walk you through install,
initialization, and platform authorization. Onboarding is a
conversation, not a tutorial.

Why no end-to-end content generation?

Content capabilities ship as APIs and Skills that your agent calls.
The steering wheel stays with you. Tools that try to replace agents
will pass; infrastructure that powers agents is just beginning.

Will multi-account operation get me banned?

OmniMux executes at a human pace, with configurable limits and full
audit logs. We treat platform rules as constraints, not obstacles to
route around. Final enforcement decisions always rest with the
platforms.

Which channels does OmniMux support?

Global social platforms through one API—create and orchestrate. The channel matrix grows without a hard cap.

Which agents and runtimes are supported?

Any MCP-compatible client works, and the OmniMux CLI and REST API are
always available. Agents like Claude Code and Kimi are ready once the
Skill is installed.

How is usage billed?

Free to install, pay per call—no subscription. Every action lists its
credit price. Estimate before you call, see the cost in every response,
and cap spend per API key.

Who owns the content and data?

You do. Content belongs to the account that invoked it. Logs and
artifacts can be deleted, and we do not train models on your data.
