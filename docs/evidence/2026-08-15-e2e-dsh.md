## Timeout research (before the sync change)

- `@deepseek-ai/dsh-tool-call-timeout-policy` only arms a deadline when the tool declares `timeoutMs`. Omit = no deadline (`packages/guard/timeout-policy/src/index.ts`).
- Base-bundle `timeoutMs: 60000` is session-title and bash-sandbox, not a global tool execute cap.
- Headless waits `agent.whenIdle()` with no runner timeout.

Sync generate of 2–4 minutes is allowed. `drama_generate_shot` does not set `timeoutMs`.
